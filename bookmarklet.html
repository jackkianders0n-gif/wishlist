Wishlist bookmarklet

← Back to the wishlist

A button for your bookmarks bar. On any product page, click it to grab the picture, name and price, even on shops like Argos that block the server.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: ⌘ Shift B on Mac, Ctrl Shift B on Windows (Safari: View › Show Favourites Bar).
  2. Drag this red button onto the bar:
+ Wishlist

  1. Open a product page (try an Argos one) and click + Wishlist in the bar.
  2. If you change the settings above, drag the button again and delete the old one.
